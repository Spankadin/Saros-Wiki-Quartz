Saros is an original fantasy campaign setting created and owned by **James McBroom**.

## Inspirations

Saros is not dependent on Old-School Essentials, but its play assumptions are heavily inspired by OSE and the wider old-school tradition. Other strong influences of mine include OD\&D, AD\&D, BECMI, Mystara, Basic Fantasy, the _Worlds Without…_ series, _Star Trek_, _Star Wars_, _BSG_, _Hellraiser_, _Alien_, _Labyrinth_, _Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood_, _Morrowind_, _Fallout_, _Final Fantasy_, _Dune_, _Elden Ring_, Douglas Adams, Robert Anton Wilson, Studio Ghibli, and most heavily of all - J. R. R. Tolkien.

[[Zixu]] and [[Serpent Ruin]] began as Saros adaptations of Skerples' [_Tomb of the Serpent Kings_](https://coinsandscrolls.blogspot.com/2018/10/osr-tomb-of-serpent-kings-v4.html). They have since been integrated into Saros's own history and geography; the original adventure and its third-party material retain their respective ownership and license.

## Creative ownership

All original Saros ideas, characters, locations, settings, factions, stories, maps, artwork, adventures, and quests presented on this wiki are works by James McBroom. The realm and its creative identity are distinct from the software used to publish this website.

## Site and vault curation

This website is generated with the open-source Quartz publishing framework from James' Obsidian vault. AI-assisted tools help curate, organize, edit, cross-link, and technically maintain the vault and public wiki under James's direction. That assistance is editorial and technical: the original ideas, characters, settings, and quests are wholly the creation of James McBroom.

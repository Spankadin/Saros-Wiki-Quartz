The [[Crossroads Council]] is the civic government of [[Trystn]].

Its responsibilities reflect the town's purpose as a road and caravan hub.

## Responsibilities

The Council oversees:

- Road maintenance
- Toll schedules
- Caravan yards
- Market rules
- Bridge and culvert repair
- Escort licensing
- Traffic during major trade periods
- Disputes between traveling merchants

[[Elian Thorne]] serves as Roadwarden and presides from [[Tollhouse Commons]].

## Political Character

The Council tends to judge policy by whether people and goods can still move.

It is less interested in ceremony than in keeping one delayed caravan from blocking every road behind it.

The [[Dun'gan]] are remembered as the earliest dwarves of [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]]. In Sarosian Dwarvish, **Dun** (pronounced “dune”) means **dwarves** or **the dwarven people**.

Little direct history survives.

## Legacy

Dwarven tradition credits the Dun'gan with beginning [[The Wandering]], the great period of exploration during which dwarven communities spread across the mountains and discovered powerful [[Fey-Lines]].

Broken chronicles also credit their deepest expeditions with discoveries later inherited, hidden, or fought over by the emerging clans. References to a **Deep Jewel** survive, but their meaning is disputed.

As those communities became more isolated and specialized, new [[Dwarven Clans]] developed.

The original Dun'gan identity gradually disappeared.

No modern settlement openly claims to be a surviving pure Dun'gan community, though nearly every major clan traces some part of its ancestry back to them.

Their disappearance is considered cultural rather than a single recorded extinction.

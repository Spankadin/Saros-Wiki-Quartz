The [[Dun'zod]] are the **Mountain Keepers** and **Gem-Seekers**, a [[Dwarven Clans|dwarven clan]] formed from traditions once recorded separately as the Tha'run and Vak'dar. In Sarosian Dwarvish, **Dun** (pronounced “dune”) means **dwarves** or **the dwarven people**.

They are the dwarves most frequently seen in the settled parts of Saros, although Dun'zod communities reveal themselves, open their gates, and trade only when they choose.

## Specialty

Dun'zod craft focuses on:

- Gem cutting
- Magical stone
- Surveying fey-line convergences
- Deep mining
- [[Fey-stone]]
- Structural reinforcement
- Underground roads, ventilation, and water control
- Defensive gates and deliberate sealing

Dwarven tradition credits their forebears with developing many of the practices that later became known simply as **delving**. Their engineers are equally respected for knowing when a dangerous depth must be sealed.

## Reputation

Dun'zod traders, engineers, and envoys are encountered throughout Saros, but outsiders rarely see the clan's deeper holdings. Stories about Dun'zod works often end with a sealed door and no explanation.

Old Dun'zod markings are especially prized because they can identify magical deposits, stable underground routes, or places that should never be opened again.

The [[Khaz'um]] are remembered as the earliest dwarves of [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]].

Little direct history survives.

## Legacy

Dwarven tradition credits the Khaz'um with beginning [[The Wandering]], the great period of exploration during which dwarven communities spread across the mountains and discovered powerful [[Fey-Lines]].

Broken chronicles also credit their deepest expeditions with discoveries later inherited, hidden, or fought over by the emerging clans. References to a **Deep Jewel** survive, but their meaning is disputed.

As those communities became more isolated and specialized, new [[Dwarven Clans]] developed.

The original Khaz'um identity gradually disappeared.

No modern settlement openly claims to be a surviving pure Khaz'um community, though nearly every major clan traces some part of its ancestry back to them.

Their disappearance is considered cultural rather than a single recorded extinction.

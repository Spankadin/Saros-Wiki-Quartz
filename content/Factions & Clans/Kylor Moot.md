The [[Kylor Moot]] is the practical civic government of [[Kylor]].

Logging families, fishers, boat owners, timber factors, and experienced craftspeople dominate its decisions.

[[Derran Pike]] serves as Harbor Reeve.

## Business

The Moot handles:

- Timber cutting rights
- Harbor rules
- Fishing disputes
- Fire precautions
- Coastal watch obligations
- Logging-road maintenance
- Contracts affecting communal forest access

Formal meetings are held when necessary.

Much of Kylor's real negotiation happens around [[Saltwood Market]], where everyone affected by a decision eventually has to do business.

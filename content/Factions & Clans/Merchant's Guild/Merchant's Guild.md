The [[Merchant's Guild]] is one of the broadest commercial organizations in [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]].

It does not control all trade, but membership provides recognition, arbitration, contacts, and a shared set of expectations that make long-distance business less dangerous.

## Functions

The Guild commonly deals with:

- Trade standards
- Weights and measures
- Contract witnessing
- Merchant arbitration
- Caravan partnerships
- Credit references
- Warehousing connections
- Information about dangerous routes

Its influence is strongest in commercial centers such as [[Goslow]], [[Port Quartz]], [[Trystn]], and [[Soryn's Crossing]].

## Appearance and Habits

Guild members generally aim to look prosperous, trustworthy, and professionally ornate. Clean layered clothing, quality fabric, well-kept boots, practical traveling coats, stamped seals, ledgers, keys, measuring tools, and restrained jewelry communicate that a merchant can protect both goods and reputation.

The exact display varies by trade. A caravan factor dresses for weather and roads; a hall broker may favor richer fabric and careful ornament. Obvious wealth is useful, but appearing careless with it is not.

## Politics

The Guild usually prefers stable government because stable roads make money.

That does not mean it blindly supports the Crown.

When tariffs, military seizures, or local corruption threaten commerce, powerful Guild members can resist through delayed shipments, legal challenges, private guards, and coordinated refusal to extend credit.

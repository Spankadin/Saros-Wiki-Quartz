The [[Mooring Council]] is the chartered civic government of [[Port Quartz]].

It regulates ferries, salvage, warehouses, tariffs, guide licenses, emergency stores, and ordinary civic justice.

[[Hesta Vane]] serves as First Mooring and presides from [[Hall of Moorings]].

The council recognizes the Crown but strongly resists attempts by the [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] to convert emergency military authority into permanent control of the town.

## Seats and Influence

The Council's traditional offices divide responsibility among the people who keep the river town functioning: ferry operations, river works, merchant factors, civic wards, and the First Mooring.

Wealth matters in Port Quartz, but so does the ability to keep ferries moving during bad water. A rich merchant who cannot solve a practical river problem may carry less influence than an experienced crew leader.

[[Hesta Vane]] chairs the Council without treating it as a personal court.

The [[Stone Council]] governs [[Lilya]] and represents the town’s mine crews, smelters, haulers, craftsmen, and established households.

[[Maela Torr]] serves as Stone Reeve and chairs meetings at [[Stonewake Hall]].

The council cooperates with the [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] on defense but treats local mine law as a civic right rather than a privilege granted by the Crown.

## Authority

The Council sets mine closures, approves major hauling contracts, maintains emergency food and timber reserves, and settles disputes that cross several mining crews.

Its rulings matter because a careless decision underground can endanger an entire district rather than one household.

[[Maela Torr]] chairs meetings but is expected to persuade the other interests rather than simply command them.

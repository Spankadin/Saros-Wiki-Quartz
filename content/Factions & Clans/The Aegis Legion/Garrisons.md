Major known [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] garrisons include:

- [[Stonepeak]] in [[Pyrris]] — commanded by [[General Ironclad]]
- [[Duskpoint]] outside [[Goslow]] — commanded by [[Vostin The Mighty]]
- [[Mira's Wall]] in [[Grymswatch]] — naval stronghold of [[Admiral Thainan]]
- The Port Quartz garrison — commanded by [[Captain Serin Holt]]

Smaller posts, road stations, signal towers, and temporary patrol camps exist throughout the settled regions of [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]].

The Legion's legal authority differs according to local charter. A garrison may be militarily dominant without serving as the town's civil government.

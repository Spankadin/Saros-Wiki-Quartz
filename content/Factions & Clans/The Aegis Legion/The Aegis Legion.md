[[The Aegis Legion]] is the principal military institution of the Crown and the most visible symbol of royal authority outside [[Pyrris]].

Its motto is:

> **Honor, Heart, Homeland**

## Purpose

The Legion is responsible for:

- Defense of the realm
- Garrisons in major settlements
- Patrols along important roads
- Coastal and naval security
- Response to monsters or organized raiders
- Enforcement of serious Crown law
- Support during disasters and sieges

In many remote places, the arrival of a Legion patrol is the only regular contact people have with the central government.

## Appearance and Equipment

The Legion presents itself as practical, disciplined, and professional. Armor, clothing, weapons, and insignia are maintained for hard use rather than ceremonial spectacle, although officers and formal guards may wear more polished equipment.

[[Griffin Riders]] use lighter functional riding armor, secure harness points, weather protection, and gear designed not to interfere with wings or saddle movement. A griffin and rider are lifelong partners, not interchangeable cavalry equipment issued from a stable.

## Command

[[General Ironclad]] is the supreme military commander.

Important regional officers include:

- [[Vostin The Mighty]] at [[Duskpoint]] near [[Goslow]]
- [[Admiral Thainan]] at [[Grymswatch]]
- [[Captain Serin Holt]] at [[Port Quartz]]

The Legion's authority varies by local charter. A garrison may be powerful without technically being the civil government.

## Reputation

Supporters view the Legion as the reason roads remain open and towns survive attacks from the wilderness.

Critics see an armed institution increasingly willing to decide what the law means for itself.

Both views have become stronger since [[Queen Amara]] disappeared.

Without the Queen's personal authority, Legion commanders are receiving more responsibility while civic leaders, guilds, and [[The Black Circle]] question how temporary those powers really are.

## Garrisons

The largest concentration of Legion forces is associated with [[Grymswatch]] and the military establishment at [[Mira's Wall]].

Smaller garrisons and posts protect major roads, ports, and politically important settlements throughout the realm.

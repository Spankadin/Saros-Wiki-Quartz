[[Clarissa]] is a young human mage and known member of [[The Black Circle]].

She favors dark traveling robes, keeps her blond hair practical, and has an unassuming smile that has repeatedly failed to convince Legion officers she is harmless.

## Role

### Skaggerty

Clarissa was formally banished from [[Skaggerty]] after her connection to the Black Circle became impossible for local authorities to ignore.

She still knows people there and is likely to hear village news even when she cannot enter openly.

## Reputation

[[The Aegis Legion]] considers Clarissa troublesome because she is willing to interfere when she believes an order violates basic freedoms.

She is not famous for indiscriminate violence.

Her danger comes from mobility, magic, sympathetic contacts, and a complete lack of respect for an order merely because someone in uniform delivered it.

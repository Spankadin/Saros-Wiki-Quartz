[[The House Table]] is the governing council of independent [[Rore]].

The council is dominated by great gaming houses, lenders, brokers, ward representatives, and commercial interests that agree on one principle above all others: Rore should not be governed by the Crown.

[[Cassia Rook]] currently serves as First Dealer.

The House Table meets at [[The Dealhouse]] and funds common ward guards while allowing individual houses to maintain private security.

## How Decisions Are Made

The House Table prefers bargains to decrees.

Major gaming houses, lenders, ward interests, and commercial brokers trade support until enough of the city benefits from the same decision to make enforcement practical.

[[Cassia Rook]] presides as First Dealer, but a First Dealer who repeatedly damages business can lose support quickly.

The system is messy, wealthy, and deliberately unlike Crown government.

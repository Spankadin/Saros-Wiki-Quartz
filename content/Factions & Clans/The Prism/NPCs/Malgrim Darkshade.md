[[Malgrim Darkshade]] is the most frequently named lieutenant of [[Silas Voss]] and is commonly described as second only to him within [[The Prism]]. She is the highest-ranking operative whose continued activity is known beyond the cult.

## Appearance

Malgrim is a tall, pale elf who favors black layered clothing, restrained faceted adornment, and a deep hood.

Her white hair, milk-white eyes, and faintly refractive skin make her memorable to the few people who claim to have seen her closely.

## Reputation

Stories describe Malgrim as:

- Highly intelligent
- Patient
- Skilled in forbidden or obscure magic
- Willing to use other people as tools
- More interested in knowledge and influence than devotion for its own sake

Even people who believe Silas is the true center of the Prism often describe Malgrim as the person most likely to understand the cult's complicated magical work.

## Silas Voss

Malgrim publicly serves [[Silas Voss]], although no reliable account establishes that she has communicated with him since [[Caelum's End]].

Rumor portrays the relationship as loyalty mixed with ambition.

Whether she truly intends to surpass her master or whether that story is simply how outsiders explain her independence is unknown.

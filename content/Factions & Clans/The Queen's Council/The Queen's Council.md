[[The Queen's Council]] is the five-member body that manages much of the daily government of [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]] from [[The Radiant Citadel]] in [[Pyrris]].

Even before [[Queen Amara]] disappeared, it was an open secret that the Council handled most of the machinery that actually kept the realm functioning.

## Responsibilities

The five seats divide the broad work of government:

- [[General Ironclad]] — military command and defense
- [[Archmage Starweaver]] — arcane affairs
- [[Lord Goldhaven]] — treasury and finance
- [[Ambassador Skylark]] — diplomacy and public relations
- [[Spymaster Shadowcloak]] — intelligence and espionage

Amara traditionally stood above these offices as monarch and final authority.

## Public Reputation

The Council has never inspired the personal affection that Amara often did.

Supporters argue that five specialists have kept a difficult realm functioning across enormous distances and dangerous country.

Critics argue that the Queen's kindness allowed unelected officials to accumulate power with very little public scrutiny.

Both sides agree that the Council does a great deal more than simply offer advice.

## The Queen's Absence

Since Amara vanished, the Council has continued issuing decrees and directing the government in her name.

Some citizens praise that continuity.

Others believe the Council is:

- Concealing what happened
- Using the crisis to enlarge its authority
- Unable to control the increasingly independent Legion
- Directly responsible for the Queen's disappearance

No accusation has been publicly proven.

The longer Amara remains missing, the harder it becomes for the Council to rely on her legitimacy.

The **Scales of Weights and Way**, commonly called [[The Scales]], is one of the broadest commercial organizations in [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]]. Its formal maxim is **“By weight, by way, by word; all things find their measure.”** Most people shorten it to **“All things find their measure.”**

It does not control all trade, but membership provides recognition, arbitration, contacts, and a shared set of expectations that make long-distance business less dangerous.

## Functions

The Scales commonly deals with:

- Trade standards
- Weights and measures
- Contract witnessing
- Merchant arbitration
- Caravan partnerships
- Credit references
- Commercial loans and cargo finance
- Auction brokerage
- Warehousing connections
- Information about dangerous routes

Its influence is strongest in commercial centers such as [[Goslow]], [[Port Quartz]], [[Trystn]], and [[Soryn's Crossing]].

The Scales does not operate a branch hall in every settlement. Most members work from their own shops, warehouses, caravans, counting rooms, and market stalls. The guild provides a shared commercial network rather than a chain of uniform offices.

## Headquarters at Soryn's Crossing

The Scales maintains its headquarters at [[The Great Crossmarket]] in [[Soryn's Crossing]]. Its central exchange is a large auction house and trade hub where bulk cargo, caravan contracts, warehouse lots, and difficult claims can be inspected and sold.

Guild members travel there for major arbitrated negotiations, standards hearings, credit disputes, and meetings that require respected witnesses from several trades. The headquarters can arrange loans, escrow, appraisals, and commercial credit, but it is not a public bank. Across Saros, the only widely recognized inter-settlement banking network belongs to [[The Cast Lot]].

## Appearance and Habits

Members of the Scales generally aim to look prosperous, trustworthy, and professionally ornate. Clean layered clothing, quality fabric, well-kept boots, practical traveling coats, stamped seals, ledgers, keys, measuring tools, and restrained jewelry communicate that a merchant can protect both goods and reputation.

The exact display varies by trade. A caravan factor dresses for weather and roads; a hall broker may favor richer fabric and careful ornament. Obvious wealth is useful, but appearing careless with it is not.

## Politics

The Scales usually prefers stable government because stable roads make money.

That does not mean it blindly supports the Crown.

When tariffs, military seizures, or local corruption threaten commerce, powerful members of the Scales can resist through delayed shipments, legal challenges, private guards, and coordinated refusal to extend credit.

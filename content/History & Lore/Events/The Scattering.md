[[The Scattering]] is the name given to the flight and dispersal of the surviving [[Dun'armok]] houses after [[The Great Betrayal]].

It is distinct from [[The Wandering]]. The Wandering was the earlier expansion of the united [[Dun'gan]] and the formation of the later [[Dwarven Clans]]. The Scattering was a forced diaspora caused by civil war, ruined holds, broken alliances, and the loss of safe deep roads.

Most accounts send the surviving houses toward [[The Glimmering Expanse]]. Whether they died there, passed through it, or established hidden settlements remains one of Saros's great dwarven mysteries.

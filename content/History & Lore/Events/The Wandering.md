[[The Wandering]] is the traditional name for the era when the earliest remembered dwarves, the [[Dun'eld]], spread outward to chart and settle the deeper places of [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]].

## Expansion

Dwarven explorers discovered powerful [[Fey-Lines]] and rich mineral regions throughout the mountains.

Families and expedition groups began settling near the most promising sites.

Over generations, those settlements developed distinct customs, specialties, and identities.

The old Dun'eld identity gradually gave way to the [[Dwarven Clans]] remembered today.

## Delves

The Wandering saw the foundation of many early [[Delves]].

At first, cooperation between settlements was common. Dwarven builders, miners, gem-workers, and surveyors shared knowledge across large distances.

That unity weakened as individual clans grew wealthy and protective of their discoveries.

The period of broad cooperation ended most dramatically with [[The Great Betrayal]].

The later flight of the surviving [[Dun'armok]] is called [[The Scattering]]. It should not be confused with the Wandering: one was an age of expansion and settlement, while the other was a forced diaspora after civil war.

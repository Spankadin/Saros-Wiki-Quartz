[[Grave Asters]] are pale, star-shaped flowers that thrive in cool soil around old burial grounds, ruined chapels, and stone that has sheltered the dead.

## Uses

Across Saros, mourners leave grave asters at tombs as a promise that the deceased will be remembered without being called back. Petals are also dried for funeral incense, mourning sachets, and mild sleeping draughts.

The flowers are not poisonous, but concentrated pollen can make a breathing creature calm, drowsy, and unusually receptive to dreams.

## Folklore

Healthy grave asters are sometimes taken as evidence that a grave is quiet. Sudden blackening, blooming out of season, or flowers turning toward a living visitor are treated as warnings that something beneath the earth remains unsettled.

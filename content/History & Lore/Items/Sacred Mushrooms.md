**Sacred mushrooms** are a broad name used for several rare fungi employed in certain Sarosian divination practices.

The preparations are not ordinary food and are usually handled by experienced sages, priests, herbalists, or ritual specialists.

[[Ostalda]] of [[Temple of Torm]] is known for years of divinatory use.

Her skin has developed a distinctive blue cast that locals attribute to repeated exposure.

Different traditions disagree over whether the mushrooms reveal genuine prophecy, heighten sensitivity to [[Fey-Lines]], or simply place the mind in a state where meaningful patterns can be recognized.

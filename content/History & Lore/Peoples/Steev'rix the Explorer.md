[[Steev'rix the Explorer]] is the traveler and artist commissioned by [[Perla Mudridge]] to draw [[Map of Saros|Perla's Map of Saros]] for display in [[The Toad]].

Steev'rix worked from personal journeys, sailors' accounts, road itineraries, older charts, and stories supplied by people who rarely agreed with one another. The resulting map is respected for its scope and character, but it was never claimed to be a precise survey. Its five-mile hexes are reliable for campaign planning only when roads, terrain, waterways, and a reasonable allowance for error are also considered.

Steev'rix's current whereabouts are unknown. His last public remarks concerned a tip that might reveal the location of the dwarves' legendary **Deep Jewel**. No dependable message from him followed.

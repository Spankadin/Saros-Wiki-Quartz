[[Smoke Leaf]] is an aromatic cultivated plant grown throughout Saros. Its dried leaves and flowers are smoked, steeped into bitter tea, or baked into small confections for relaxation, pain relief, appetite, and convivial use.

Use is common among many classes and peoples. Customs vary by settlement, but most Sarosians treat smoke leaf much as they treat ale: ordinary in moderation, foolish before dangerous work, and a concern for local law only when it causes a public nuisance or neglect of duty.

## Trade

Nearly every agricultural region produces a local variety. Quality depends on seed, soil, curing, and the patience of the grower.

It is widely accepted that the finest smoke leaf comes from the halfling leaf farms around [[Mysha]]. Myshan growers guard family seed lines, cure their harvest in low cedar sheds, and sell named blends at prices that encourage counterfeiting elsewhere.

Smoke leaf travels well when kept dry. It is a common caravan good, tavern purchase, informal gift, and taxable luxury in towns that need easy revenue.

The [[Albyrt Grain Steps]] are broad stone terraces descending toward the lake, designed so carts can unload grain directly into barges and ferries. On busy days the steps become one of the loudest places in [[Albyrt]].

## Everyday Use

Trade here follows the rhythms of arrivals and departures rather than a fixed modern schedule. Prices can change sharply when a caravan, fishing fleet, or shipment is late, and local information often moves between stalls faster than it reaches any official notice board.

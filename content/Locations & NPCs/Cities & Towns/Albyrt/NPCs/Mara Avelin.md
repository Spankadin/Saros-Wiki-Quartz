[[Mara Avelin]] is the current Lake Reeve of [[Albyrt]], responsible for balancing ferry interests, grain merchants, shoreline households, and the constant civic comparison with [[Isoltide]].

Mara is measured and patient in public, with the habit of asking for cargo numbers before entertaining grand political arguments. She came from a ferry-owning family and still knows the lake well enough to embarrass captains who exaggerate the weather.

She considers cooperation with Isoltide essential but dislikes any agreement that makes Albyrt appear secondary.

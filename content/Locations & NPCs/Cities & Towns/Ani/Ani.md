[[Ani]] is a fortified coastal town whose primary purpose is to protect settled southern Saros from dangers associated with [[The Urmong Barrows]].

Fishing keeps the town fed, but defense defines its identity.

## Character

Ani is watchful. Gates close early, watch fires are maintained regardless of weather, and local children learn the alarm signals before they learn most prayers.

Travelers heading toward the Barrows are common. Travelers returning from them are memorable.

## Government

Ani operates under a **March Charter** that grants its defensive officers unusual local authority. [[Captain Lysa Dorn]] currently serves as March Warden.

The town cooperates closely with the [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] but retains its own militia and watch tradition.

## Trade and Economy

- Fishing
- Salted provisions
- Armor and equipment repair
- Guide and escort services
- Supplies for expeditions toward the Barrows
- Coastal trade

## Travel and Connections

Ani is tied to southern Saros by coastal traffic and guarded roads, while [[Barrowgate]] controls the dangerous inland approach toward [[The Urmong Barrows]]. No dependable mileage for those expedition routes has yet been fixed.

See [[Saros - Distances, Travel, Trade, and Population]] for the current map scale and travel assumptions.

## Security

Ani has strong walls for its size, layered gates, coastal patrols, and watch towers facing inland toward the Barrows.

## Places of Interest

- [[Barrowgate]] — heavily fortified inland gate
- [[The Last Lantern]] — expedition inn and gathering place
- [[Ani Watchhouse]] — militia headquarters and warning tower

[[The Last Lantern]] is the traditional gathering place for expeditions preparing to leave [[Ani]] toward [[The Urmong Barrows]]. Equipment changes hands constantly, and the walls carry memorial marks for groups that never returned.

## Everyday Use

The establishment serves as more than a place to drink or sleep. Travelers use it to hear road conditions, find temporary help, arrange meetings, and learn which local businesses are actually open to strangers.

## Tradition

Groups heading toward [[The Urmong Barrows]] often leave a name, mark, or short note in the Lantern before departure.

Some are crossed out when the travelers return.

Others remain for years.

The practice is voluntary, but the collection has become an informal memorial and a useful record of who went into the Barrows.

[[The Copper Gull]] is a busy [[Ari]] inn shared by fishers, grain merchants, and prospectors. The common room displays ore samples beside mounted fish, and arguments about which profession lies more are never resolved.

## Everyday Use

The establishment serves as more than a place to drink or sleep. Travelers use it to hear road conditions, find temporary help, arrange meetings, and learn which local businesses are actually open to strangers.

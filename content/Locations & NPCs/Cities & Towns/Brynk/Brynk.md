[[Brynk]] is a small and peculiar mountain town whose isolation has preserved laws, rituals, and customs that often seem baffling to outsiders.

The settlement sits high in the southern range and does not encourage casual immigration.

## Character

Brynk is quiet, enclosed, and deliberate. Doors are painted with family marks, public behavior is tightly regulated by custom, and visitors are expected to learn local rules quickly rather than plead ignorance later.

## Government

The town is governed by a closed council of established households. [[Vessa Keln]] serves as the current **Voice of Brynk**, but the title grants less personal freedom than outsiders assume.

Brynk recognizes the Crown in principle while insisting that local customary law governs nearly everything within its walls.

## Trade and Economy

Brynk trades selectively in mountain herbs, wool, preserved foods, stonework, and a few specialized crafts.

The town's small population means it relies on periodic caravans for salt, metal goods, grain, and luxuries.

## Travel and Connections

[[Soryn's Crossing]] lies roughly **40-50 road miles** away: 2-3 days walking, 3-4 by wagon, about 2 days mounted, or less than a day by griffin.

The mountain climb raises the price of cheap bulky goods and helps preserve Brynk's isolation. See [[Saros - Distances, Travel, Trade, and Population]] for the canonical route model.

## Security

The mountain itself provides much of Brynk's defense. Narrow approaches, watch posts, and a population that knows every local trail make surprise attack difficult.

## Places of Interest

- [[The Closed Gate]] — the formal entrance where visitors receive local rules
- [[House of Measures]] — civic hall and court of custom
- [[The Quiet Cup]] — the only inn routinely permitted to host outsiders

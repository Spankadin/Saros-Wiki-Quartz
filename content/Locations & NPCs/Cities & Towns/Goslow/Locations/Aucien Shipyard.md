[[Aucien Shipyard]] is the principal Aucien family marina and shipyard on [[Bishop's Bay]], just north of the main walls of [[Goslow]].

The yard is larger and rougher than the family's in-town business at [[The Bulkhead]].

## Work

The shipyard handles:

- Hull repair
- Small-vessel construction
- Rigging replacement
- Timber storage
- Haul-out work
- Dock space
- Custom marine jobs

The family's rope and shipbuilding experience allows work to move between the yard and the Bulkhead depending on the size of the job.

## Position

Its location outside the main gates gives the yard more shoreline and working space than could be found inside Goslow, but it also means the family must take its own security seriously after dark.

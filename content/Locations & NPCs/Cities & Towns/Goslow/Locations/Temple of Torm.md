[[Temple of Torm]] is one of the most respected religious buildings in [[Goslow]], serving as a temple, library, place of counsel, and refuge.

[[Korth]] is the elderly head priest.

[[Ostalda]] lives in a small chamber connected to the temple library and works there as a sage and diviner.

## Korth

Korth is now famous for falling asleep at inconvenient moments, but older residents still tell stories of the paladin who rallied Goslow against a major orc raid.

His age has diminished his stamina more than his reputation.

## Library

The temple library contains:

- Religious works
- Local histories
- Old travel accounts
- Copies of legal and civic records
- Material collected by priests and adventurers

Ostalda's presence has also made it a place people visit when ordinary scholarship is not enough.

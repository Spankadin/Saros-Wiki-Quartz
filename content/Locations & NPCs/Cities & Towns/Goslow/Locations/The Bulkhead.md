[[The Bulkhead]] is the Aucien family's long-running rope and shipbuilding business in [[Goslow]].

[[Jarvis Aucien]] oversees the shop.

## Work

The Bulkhead specializes in:

- Rope
- Rigging
- Blocks and tackle
- Sail and line repair
- Ship fittings
- Small marine construction
- Contract work for larger vessels

Large hull work can be transferred to [[Aucien Shipyard]] outside the city walls.

## Family Business

The business has passed through the Aucien family for generations and is closely associated with Goslow's working waterfront.

Jarvis's size, blond hair, and muscular build make him difficult to miss in a shop already full of coils, spars, timber, and dockworkers.

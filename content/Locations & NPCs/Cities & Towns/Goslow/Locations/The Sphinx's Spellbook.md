[[The Sphinx's Spellbook]] is an old, narrow two-story apothecary and arcane shop only a short walk from the [[Goslow]] docks.

The cracked stone building is threaded with roots and vines that seem to have grown through it rather than around it.

## Interior

Incense, herbal smoke, dust, parchment, potion bottles, books, charms, and strange collected objects fill almost every available surface.

A narrow path through the clutter eventually reaches a small counter and bell.

## Mori

[[Mori]] owns the shop.

She is a timid but extremely intelligent young half-elf with purple hair and alchemical tattoos covering much of her visible skin.

Mori lives above the shop with [[Chibs]], a penguin she rescued injured and alone near [[Bishop's Bay]].

She crafted the small frost-rune pendant Chibs wears to help keep him comfortable in Goslow's climate.

## Goods

The Spellbook is a place to look for:

- Herbs
- Alchemical ingredients
- Minor magical supplies
- Unusual books
- Potions
- Oddments collected from travelers

What Mori actually has in stock changes constantly.

[[The Storm Runner]] is a small troop transport captained by [[Mirken Stanton]] and navigated by [[Breelyn]].

The vessel carries a regular complement of **24 officers and crew**. Her relatively light build, disciplined sail handling, and shallow-enough working draft make her faster and less cumbersome than a major troopship, while remaining capable of deep-sea passages.

On the [[Goslow]]–[[Port Quartz]] run, the crew can sail and tack when the wind favors them, then warp between hauling posts, kedge with the ship's boats, or tow through short difficult approaches. For sustained upstream cordelling, she contracts licensed pilots and bank-hauling crews along the prepared reaches. None of this is effortless: working upriver consumes money and labor, reduces useful cargo or troop capacity, and can force the ship to wait on wind, current, or available river hands.

A normal passage to Port Quartz takes about **5–8 days upstream** and **2–4 days returning downstream**. A practical round trip is **10–16 days** once loading and ordinary weather are included.

## Crew

[[Mirken Stanton|Mirken]] commands the ship with a mixture of confidence, aggression, and personal loyalty to his people.

[[Breelyn]], his childhood friend and navigator, is regarded by Mirken as the finest reader of the waters and stars he has ever known.

The remaining complement normally includes two mates, a boatswain, carpenter, sailmaker-rigger, cook-steward, and sixteen sailors and deckhands. Soldiers, passengers, and contracted river laborers are carried in addition to that core complement when space and supplies allow.

## Reputation

The Storm Runner is associated with hard sailing, dangerous routes, and a crew that does not particularly enjoy being ordered around by the [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]].

Mirken's open hostility toward [[Admiral Thainan]] is well known among people familiar with the ship.

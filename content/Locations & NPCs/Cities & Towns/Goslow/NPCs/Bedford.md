Overseeing [[Bedford's Forge]] is a massive man simply known as Bedford. He has a loud booming voice, a shiny bald head, and a large grey mustache. He and his wife [[Mary]] love visitors and they are always cheerful. His leather overalls are covered in soot and burn marks and his hands are like chiseled steel from decades of dedicated smith-work. Though very bulky and freakishly strong, he is also a proficient artist who adorns his creations with delicate and detailed inlays. He is one of the few beings in all of [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]] capable of forging [[Fey-stone]].

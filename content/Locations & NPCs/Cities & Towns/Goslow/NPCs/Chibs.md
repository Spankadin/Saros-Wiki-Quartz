[[Chibs]] is the tiny penguin who lives with [[Mori]] above [[The Sphinx's Spellbook]] in [[Goslow]].

Mori found him alone, injured, and badly out of place near [[Bishop's Bay]] several years ago. She nursed him back to health and eventually built a small frost-rune pendant that helps keep his body cool in Goslow's climate.

## Appearance

_Not yet established._

## Personality

### Behavior

Chibs waddles freely through the shop and has learned exactly which shelves he is allowed to bump into without causing a disaster.

The pendant leaves him cool enough that small beads of condensation and meltwater sometimes appear where he has been sitting.

Regular customers tend to know Chibs almost as well as they know Mori.

## Role

Chibs serves as **Shop Companion**.

## Reputation

Nobody agrees how a penguin ended up alone in Bishop's Bay.

Mori has stopped pretending she knows.

_No additional structured relationship is currently recorded._

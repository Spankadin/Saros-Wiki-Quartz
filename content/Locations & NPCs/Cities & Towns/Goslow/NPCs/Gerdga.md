[[Gerdga]] is the manager of [[The Toad]] and the person most likely to actually know what is happening inside the enormous inn at any given moment.

She is a short halfling with a deep raspy voice, a terrible smoker's cough, and a dry sarcasm that regulars learn to appreciate or at least survive.

## Appearance

_Not yet established._

## Personality

### Habits

Gerdga is usually seen with:

- A pipe in one hand
- A magnifying glass close by
- A ledger she insists nobody else keeps correctly

She examines every unfamiliar coin before accepting it and has embarrassed more than one traveler who tried to pass damaged or questionable currency.

## Role

Gerdga serves as **Manager**.

## Reputation

_Not yet established._

## Relationships

[[Perla Mudridge]] is Gerdga's closest friend as well as the owner of The Toad.

Gerdga manages staff, tabs, supplies, complaints, and most practical problems so Perla can spend more time talking to guests and collecting stories.

[[Lone-Wolf Louie]] oversees [[The Silver Drake]], the famous hall of [[The Cast Lot]] and bank in [[Goslow]].

He speaks with a heavy accent and is almost always chewing something, which makes him difficult to understand even when he is trying to be clear.

## Appearance

_Not yet established._

## Personality

Louie gives the impression of being distracted until money or cheating is involved.

At that point he becomes extremely precise.

He likes regular customers who lose gracefully, distrusts people who win too loudly, and has no patience for anyone who treats a Guild hall like an unprotected tavern game.

## Role

Louie supervises:

- Gaming-floor staff
- House security
- High-value disputes
- Banking operations
- Large withdrawals
- Relationships with the Cast Lot

He rarely deals cards himself anymore.

Munchil “[[Munchies Muldridge|Munchies]]” Muldridge works at [[The Toad]] in [[Goslow]].

He is the nephew of [[Perla Mudridge]] and has spent much of his life surrounded by adventurers, which has convinced him that he should obviously become one.

## Appearance

_Not yet established._

## Personality

Munchies is enthusiastic, clumsy, easily distracted, and not especially bright.

He volunteers for jobs before understanding them and has an impressive ability to turn a simple errand into something Perla eventually has to fix.

## Role

### At the Toad

His duties change according to whatever [[Gerdga]] believes he can perform without damaging furniture.

He carries luggage, clears tables, runs messages, helps in the stable, and occasionally disappears because he decided to “practice adventuring.”

Most regulars like him.

Very few would trust him with a secret route, expensive potion, or lit torch.

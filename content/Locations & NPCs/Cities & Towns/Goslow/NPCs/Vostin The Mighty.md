[[Vostin The Mighty]] commands the [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] garrison at [[Duskpoint]] outside [[Goslow]].

He is stern, proud, and intensely loyal to the institution he serves. Vostin is not known for warmth, but neither is he casually cruel. He believes order is one of the few things keeping the inhabited parts of Saros from collapsing back into dangerous isolation.

## The Mighty

People in Goslow call him **Vostin the Mighty** partly because of his physical confidence and partly because he is rarely seen lowering himself before anyone.

The exceptions are [[General Ironclad]] and [[Queen Amara]], both of whom receive an obedience from him that he offers almost no one else.

## Duties

Vostin oversees:

- Duskpoint's garrison
- Legion policing around Goslow
- Patrols on the roads near the city
- Assignment of the garrison's few griffin pairs during serious threats
- Military coordination with the approaches to [[The High Road]]

He takes to the air only when speed or an aerial view justifies risking one of Duskpoint's rare pairs. His appearance overhead is memorable precisely because it is uncommon.

## Griffin Bond

Vostin claimed his griffin as a hatchling in [[Vy'Tungavor]]. The animal is his lifelong bonded companion rather than Legion property, and the two remain in regular proximity even when Vostin's duties keep him at [[Duskpoint]].

Their bond, care, equipment, and travel follow the traditions described in [[Griffin Riders]].

## Reputation

Merchants appreciate that his patrols keep important routes moving. People who dislike the Legion see him as the local face of Crown power.

Vostin accepts neither opinion as especially relevant to his work.

## Relationships

He answers ultimately to [[General Ironclad]] and holds [[Queen Amara]] in unusually high personal regard.

His relationship with other Legion commanders is professional rather than friendly. [[Captain Serin Holt]] of [[Port Quartz]], in particular, considers Vostin too willing to interpret orders according to local circumstances.

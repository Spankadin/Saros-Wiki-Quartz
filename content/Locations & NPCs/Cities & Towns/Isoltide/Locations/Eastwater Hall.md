[[Eastwater Hall]] is the ferry registry and civic hall of [[Isoltide]], where [[Teren Moss]] and the local council conduct business.

## Public Use

Most residents encounter the building through ordinary civic business rather than ceremony. Notices, disputes, permits, records, and requests for local officials all pass through it, making the place a useful measure of what the settlement currently considers important.

## Lake Coordination

Eastwater Hall exchanges weather and ferry information with [[Albyrt]] across the lake.

Even during political disputes, both cities usually continue sharing storm warnings and missing-vessel reports. Nobody profits when a ferry sinks to prove a point about tolls.

[[Teren Moss]] serves as East Reeve of [[Isoltide]].

A former timber factor with deep family ties to the lakeshore, Teren is comfortable negotiating with ferrymen, fishers, foresters, and merchants who all believe their problems should be solved first.

Teren has a friendly public rivalry with [[Mara Avelin]] of [[Albyrt]]. Their arguments over tolls and ferry schedules are famous enough to be repeated in taverns, but both understand that the twin cities depend on one another.

[[Kylor]] is a lonely logging and fishing town on the northwestern edge of the settled coast. Dense forest presses close behind the town while cold water and broken shoreline hem it in from the west.

Unlike [[Goslow]], Kylor does not feel like a place people casually pass through. Most visitors came for timber, fish, work, or because they had nowhere easier to go.

## Character

The settlement smells of pitch, salt, smoke, sawdust, and wet rope.

People are accustomed to long absences. Logging crews vanish into the forest for weeks, fishing boats stay out through bad weather, and a household learning not to panic at an overdue family member is almost a local rite of passage.

## Government

The [[Kylor Moot]] governs local affairs under [[Derran Pike]], the **Harbor Reeve**.

Logging families, boat owners, fishers, and timber factors dominate civic decisions. The [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] keeps a small presence, but Kylor values practical protection far more than outside supervision.

## Trade and Economy

Kylor exports:

- Ship timber and heavy beams
- Barrel staves
- Charcoal
- Pitch and resin
- Smoked and salted fish
- Rope fiber
- Small coastal craft
- Forest products

Much of the best timber eventually reaches [[Goslow]].

## Travel and Connections

[[Goslow]] lies roughly **45-50 road miles** away: 2-3 days walking, 3-4 days by heavy wagon, or 1.5-2 days mounted. A coastal vessel can make the trip in about a day in good conditions, so the heaviest timber usually moves by water.

Commercial cargo bound for [[Port Quartz]] normally passes through Goslow and changes vessels or transport there. The full journey commonly takes **3-5 days**, depending on loading and weather.

See [[Saros - Distances, Travel, Trade, and Population]] for the canonical route model.

## Security

Kylor is protected by a stout timber palisade, coastal watch fires, armed logging crews, local boats, and a modest Legion detachment.

The most common serious threat is not an army. It is whatever emerges from the forest after a camp stops answering signals.

## Places of Interest

- [[The Drowning Mermaid]] — the oldest tavern in Kylor
- [[Blackpine Yard]] — the largest saw yard and log boom
- [[Lantern Head]] — coastal watch post and storm beacon
- [[Saltwood Market]] — fish, timber contracts, rope, pitch, and forest goods

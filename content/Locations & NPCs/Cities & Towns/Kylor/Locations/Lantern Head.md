[[Lantern Head]] is the coastal watch post above [[Kylor]].

Its beacon warns of storms, missing boats, pirate sightings, and emergencies at logging camps close enough to signal the coast.

A small [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] detachment shares the post with local watchmen.

## Security

The structure is part of the settlement's ordinary defensive routine, not merely a wartime monument. Watch rotations, inspections, signal procedures, and emergency stores are maintained even during quiet seasons.

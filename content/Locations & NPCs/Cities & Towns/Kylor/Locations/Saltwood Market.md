[[Saltwood Market]] is the commercial center of [[Kylor]] and the usual meeting place of [[Derran Pike]] and the [[Kylor Moot]] when business spills beyond formal sessions.

Fish, rope, pitch, charcoal, tools, and timber contracts dominate the stalls.

News from [[Goslow]] often arrives here several days before it reaches the logging camps.

## Everyday Use

Trade here follows the rhythms of arrivals and departures rather than a fixed modern schedule. Prices can change sharply when a caravan, fishing fleet, or shipment is late, and local information often moves between stalls faster than it reaches any official notice board.

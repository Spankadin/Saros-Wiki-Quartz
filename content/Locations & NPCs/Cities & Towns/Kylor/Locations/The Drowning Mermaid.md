[[The Drowning Mermaid]] is the oldest tavern in [[Kylor]] and the first place most outsiders hear the town’s real news.

[[Mara Venn]] runs the Mermaid with a practical tolerance for loggers, fishers, Legion patrols, and travelers who know when to stop asking questions.

The walls are covered in broken figureheads, storm-damaged signs, and pieces of boats that never returned intact.

## Everyday Use

The establishment serves as more than a place to drink or sleep. Travelers use it to hear road conditions, find temporary help, arrange meetings, and learn which local businesses are actually open to strangers.

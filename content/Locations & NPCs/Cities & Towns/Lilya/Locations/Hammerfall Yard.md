[[Hammerfall Yard]] is the loud industrial center of [[Lilya]].

Ore is weighed, sorted, broken, smelted, loaded, and argued over here. Wagons bound for [[Trystn]] form in the yard, while specially reinforced barrels intended for the [[Fall Casks|fall-cask route]] toward [[Port Quartz]] are inspected nearby.

The yard never truly becomes quiet.

## Workday

The site is busiest when goods are arriving, being processed, or being prepared for transport. Laborers, haulers, inspectors, and specialist craftspeople all depend on the work staying organized, so strangers wandering through active areas are noticed quickly.

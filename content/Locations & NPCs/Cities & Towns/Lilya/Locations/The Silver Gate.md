[[The Silver Gate]] is the most heavily controlled entrance into the primary mine works of [[Lilya]].

[[Garran Flint]] oversees access, safety records, shift closures, and the locking of shafts considered too unstable for ordinary workers.

## Structure

The gate is built into an older stone opening reinforced repeatedly by later Sarosian construction.

Equipment checks, worker tallies, and emergency bells make the entrance feel more like a fortress checkpoint than a normal mine mouth.

## Deeper Workings

Some older passages below the active mines are clearly dwarven and extend toward parts of [[The Silvervein Summits]] no current survey fully explains.

Most miners have no reason to go near them.

The named settlements below are the largest or most strategically important concentrations of people currently established in known [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]].

The wilderness between them contains many smaller villages, hamlets, farms, forts, logging camps, mines, and roadside settlements that do not appear on most continental maps.

Distances and route times are maintained in [[Saros - Distances, Travel, Trade, and Population]].

## Northern Centers

### Goslow

[[Goslow]] is the largest city in the realm and the primary commercial concentration around [[Bishop's Bay]]. Its connection to [[Pyrris]] through [[The High Road]] makes it the practical supply base of the Crown.

### Pyrris

[[Pyrris]] is a capital by status rather than population. Most permanent residents support [[The Radiant Citadel]], [[The Queen's Council]], court administration, diplomacy, or the [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]].

### Port Quartz

[[Port Quartz]] controls an important ferry crossing and the transfer of goods among western, eastern, and northern routes. Its [[Fall Casks|fall-cask trade]] is unique in Saros.

### Lilya

[[Lilya]] exists because of the mineral wealth of [[The Silvervein Summits]]. Most ordinary freight moves south to [[Trystn]].

### Kylor

[[Kylor]] is a remote working port built around fishing and the timber trade.

### Dun Karag

[[Dun Karag]] is the last permanent stronghold on the road to [[Caelum's End]]. Its [[Thar'um]] wardens maintain and control the passage through [[The Masys Mountains]].

### Rore

[[Rore]] is independent, wealthy, and politically difficult for the Crown. Gambling, lending, trade, and a sophisticated black market support its economy.

## Southern Centers

[[Albyrt]] and [[Isoltide]] form the great twin-lake trade pair.

[[Soryn's Crossing]] handles much of the south's overland and river freight.

[[Ani]] watches the dangerous country around [[The Urmong Barrows]].

[[Mysha]] is tied to logging and old halfling stories of [[Lake Runa]].

[[Ari]] supports fishing, grain, and the smaller mining claims around [[Vy'Tungavor]].

[[Brynk]] remains deliberately isolated despite its place on the map.

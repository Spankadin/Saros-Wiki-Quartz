[[The Split Log]] is a rough crew inn in [[Mysha]] where loggers and river traders eat cheaply, sleep heavily, and compare injuries.

## Everyday Use

The establishment serves as more than a place to drink or sleep. Travelers use it to hear road conditions, find temporary help, arrange meetings, and learn which local businesses are actually open to strangers.

## Clientele

The common room fills earliest when logging crews come off shift and latest when river traders are waiting on water levels.

Work is often hired informally over meals, and newcomers looking for a guide, cutter, teamster, or spare pair of hands usually ask here before visiting any formal office.

[[Mysha]] is a southern logging town set where river, forest, and open land meet. Halflings are especially common in the settlement, and many local families maintain stories connecting their ancestors to [[Lake Runa]].

## Character

Mysha is low-built, communal, and deeply tied to the forest. Sawmills and river booms sit beside gardens, workshops, and stone yards.

## Government

A small **Woodward Council** manages cutting rights, river use, fire prevention, and disputes between logging crews. [[Pellin Root]] currently acts as Speaker.

## Trade and Economy

Mysha depends primarily on logging, but also produces:

- Carved goods
- Charcoal
- River timber
- Small amounts of quarried stone
- Tools and practical crafts
- Food from nearby farms and gardens
- [[Smoke Leaf]], including the halfling-grown varieties considered the finest in Saros

Much of its timber moves by river before joining southern trade routes.

## Travel and Connections

The maintained route to [[Isoltide]] runs through [[Soryn's Crossing]]: approximately **215–235 road miles**, or 11–14 days walking, 16–21 days by heavy wagon, and 7–10 days mounted. There is no direct Isoltide–Mysha road.

[[Soryn's Crossing]] lies approximately **80–90 road miles** southwest: 4–5 days walking, 6–8 days by heavy wagon, or 3–4 days mounted. River freight may be faster when water, crew, and passage rights cooperate.

See [[Saros - Distances, Travel, Trade, and Population]] for the canonical route model.

## Security

The town relies on local wardens, organized work crews, and a militia accustomed to forest emergencies. Fire is treated as seriously as monsters.

## Places of Interest

- [[Rootwheel Mill]] — largest sawmill and river wheel
- [[Runa House]] — communal hall associated with local halfling traditions
- [[The Split Log]] — rough inn used by crews and river traders

[[Pellin Root]] is the current Speaker of [[Mysha]]'s Woodward Council.

He is a broad, weathered halfling with an unusually soft voice and a deep familiarity with timber rights, river levels, and the old family stories connecting Mysha's halflings to [[Lake Runa]].

Pellin prefers negotiated cutting boundaries over punishment. His patience ends quickly when a logging crew ignores fire restrictions or fells marked trees.

## Reputation

Pellin is well liked by crews who obey cutting rules and deeply unpopular with anyone caught burning slash carelessly. His knowledge of the river and older halfling stories makes him useful beyond ordinary council work.

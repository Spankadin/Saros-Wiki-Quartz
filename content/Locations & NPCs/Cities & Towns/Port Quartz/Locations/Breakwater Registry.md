The [[Breakwater Registry]] licenses and records specialized travel connected to [[Port Quartz]].

## Registered Professions

The office maintains records for:

- Ferry operators
- [[Fall Casks|Fall-cask]] recovery crews
- Wilderness guides
- Surveyors
- Professional escorts
- Certain river pilots

Travelers leaving established roads may also file a voluntary expedition declaration naming their route, expected return, and emergency contact.

## Public Board

The Registry posts:

- Missing travelers
- Closed ferry landings
- River conditions
- Dangerous wildlife
- Suspected bandit activity
- Guide availability
- Recent warnings from surveyors

[[Hesta Vane]] treats the registry as a civic record rather than an automatic military intelligence file.

[[North Cataract Yard]] is the exposed northern half of the paired [[Cataract Yard|Cataract Yards]] at hex **82.94**. Catch booms, hooks, cranes, sorting platforms, and emergency sheds let crews recover casks that pass the southern bank.

There are no ordinary homes and few comforts. The landing is too isolated from Port Quartz's protection and the north bank too close to unsettled country. Except during storms, accidents, or emergencies, workers take [[Westwater Ferry]] south when the suns set.

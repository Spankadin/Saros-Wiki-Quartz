The [[Quartz Vaults]] are secure storage chambers cut into pale stone above the commercial waterfront of [[Port Quartz]].

The upper chambers are leased to merchants for valuable or sensitive cargo.

Lower chambers are older than much of the present town, and several sealed doors have resisted generations of locksmiths.

## Civic Vaults

The [[Mooring Council]] maintains separate protected rooms for:

- Confiscated cargo
- Emergency grain
- Important civic records
- Hazardous salvage
- Goods held during legal disputes

The vaults are watched closely because ownership arguments become much more expensive when the object in question can fit inside a locked room.

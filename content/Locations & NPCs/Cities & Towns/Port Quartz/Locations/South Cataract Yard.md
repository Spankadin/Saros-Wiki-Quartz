[[South Cataract Yard]] is the inhabited half of the paired [[Cataract Yard|Cataract Yards]] at hex **81.95**. Its booms and catch crews recover [[Fall Casks]] from the [[Ascarid]], while its offices record brands, settle recovery fees, and hold disputed cargo.

The south bank contains the claim yard, cooperages, cranes, guarded stores, bunkhouses, family homes, kitchens, and most of the settlement's comforts. [[Westwater Ferry]] is based here and carries workers across to [[North Cataract Yard]].

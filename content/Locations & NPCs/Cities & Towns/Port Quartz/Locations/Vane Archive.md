The [[Vane Archive]] is [[Port Quartz]]'s principal collection of expedition journals, route notes, ferry records, old surveys, and disputed maps.

It began as records preserved by the family of [[Edric Vane]] and gradually absorbed material from other explorers and civic offices.

[[Nelra Vossin]] is the current keeper.

## Collections

The archive includes:

- Edric Vane's surviving expedition journals
- River-level records
- Old crossing descriptions
- Missing expedition lists
- Copies of private maps
- Merchant route notes
- Travelers' reports
- Records of places that no longer exist

## Reliability

Nelra marks sources according to how directly they can be verified.

A drunken story may still be preserved, but it is not filed beside a measured survey without explanation.

The archive is especially useful because it separates centuries-old records from recent folklore concerning [[Rom The Wizard|Rom]] and a supposed moving hill.

[[Gileon]] is the heavyset human proprietor of [[Gileon's Vault]], a dim and shabby arcane shop in [[Port Quartz]].

He is patient with hesitant buyers, protective of his stranger stock, and absolutely certain that trolls are responsible for every inconvenience in his life. Whether the claim is a private joke, a stubborn superstition, or the residue of some genuine grievance is difficult to determine.

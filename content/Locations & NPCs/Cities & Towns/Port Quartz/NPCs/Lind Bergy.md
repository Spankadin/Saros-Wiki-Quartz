[[Lind Bergy]] owns and operates [[The Quartz Cobra]], the chartered hall and banking branch of [[The Cast Lot]] in [[Port Quartz]].

Lind is quiet, immaculately organized, and gifted at remembering odds, faces, and unpaid promises. He dislikes theatrical threats because they slow the room down. A soft question from Lind usually means he already knows the answer and is measuring the lie.

## Appearance

_Not yet established._

## Personality

### Principles

Lind treats gambling as entertainment, banking as trust, and cheating as vandalism against both. He will extend a modest courtesy to a traveler with a sound reputation and refuse a fortune to someone likely to endanger the hall.

He enjoys wagers that reveal judgment rather than wealth: weather, river timing, cargo weights, and whether a proud person can walk away.

## Role

Lind Bergy serves as **Proprietor and Banker**.

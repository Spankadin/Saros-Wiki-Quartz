[[Queen's Gate]] is the guarded waterline approach beneath the cliffs of [[Pyrris]]. It is part landing, part lifting station, and part defensive checkpoint serving royal and military needs below [[The Radiant Citadel]].

It is not a public harbor or a convenient shortcut into the capital. Ordinary commercial vessels passing through [[Bishop's Bay]] keep clear of the restricted waters while Crown and [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] patrols watch the approach.

The principal public route into Pyrris remains [[The High Road]].

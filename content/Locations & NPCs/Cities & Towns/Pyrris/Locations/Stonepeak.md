[[Stonepeak]] is the principal [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] garrison in [[Pyrris]] and the command post of [[General Ironclad]].

It stands below [[The Radiant Citadel]] at the point where visitors entering the royal district can be screened before reaching more sensitive areas.

## Functions

Stonepeak contains:

- Barracks
- Offices
- Armories
- Holding rooms
- Checkpoints
- Command chambers
- Guard quarters for the capital

## The Checkpoint

Travelers entering the Citadel precinct normally pass through Stonepeak-controlled security.

Weapons, declared business, letters of introduction, and unusual cargo may all receive scrutiny.

The garrison also supports security along [[The High Road]].

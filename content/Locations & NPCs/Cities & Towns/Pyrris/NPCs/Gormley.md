[[Gormley]] is the most famous bard in [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]].

The halfling performer is known for singing, dancing, juggling, stage magic, comic storytelling, and the ability to turn a room full of people who dislike one another into an audience for at least one song.

## Role

### Touring

Gormley spends much of the year traveling between [[Major Cities, Towns, and Ports|major settlements]], performing new songs and collecting stories for the next tour.

His appearances can draw visitors from surrounding villages and are especially profitable for local taverns.

### Pyrris Residency

During the spring, Gormley traditionally resides in [[Pyrris]] for [[The Spring Harvest Festival]].

He performs public concerts and, when the royal household is functioning normally, entertains guests at private feasts in [[The Radiant Citadel]].

## Reputation

Gormley is popular enough that political figures regularly attempt to persuade him to write songs favorable to their causes.

He prefers songs that people will still want to hear after the politician is dead.

[[Salt Court]] is Rore’s neutral arbitration hall.

Debts, wagers, shipping claims, private contracts, and disputes between people who do not trust Crown courts are settled here.

A ruling from Salt Court has no royal authority, but breaking one can make every major lender and gaming house in [[Rore]] refuse a person service.

## Local Importance

The place is a familiar part of daily life and a useful point of reference for visitors. Its value comes from the ordinary work, meetings, and routines that happen there rather than from a single famous event.

[[The Black Ledger]] is a private brokerage house in [[Rore]] operated by [[Nessa Crowe]].

The public room looks respectable. The real business happens through coded ledgers, private appointments, bonded storerooms, and intermediaries.

Goods tied to pirates, forbidden magic, stolen noble property, or politically inconvenient owners can all find buyers here.

## Local Importance

The place is a familiar part of daily life and a useful point of reference for visitors. Its value comes from the ordinary work, meetings, and routines that happen there rather than from a single famous event.

[[The Crowned Die]] is the largest and most famous gambling hall in [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]]. It is [[Rore]]'s sole hall chartered by [[The Cast Lot]], the Lot's factional headquarters, and the city's legitimate bank.

[[Varo Quill]] owns and operates the Crowned Die's daily business. Its licensed tables, tournament floors, counting rooms, kitchens, guest suites, and secured vaults fill a gilt-fronted building that never completely closes.

The upper council chambers belong to the House Table. [[Cassia Rook]], the First Dealer, presides there over the Lot's senior affairs and Rore's governing coalition; Varo remains responsible for the hall below.

## Gambling

The hall hosts public tables, professional tournaments, and private games whose stakes can include ships, businesses, and contracts. The Lot's rules protect the integrity of a declared wager; they do not protect a gambler from a foolish one.

## Banking

Like every chartered Lot hall, the Crowned Die accepts deposits, honors verified drafts, changes currency, stores valuables, and arranges withdrawals through other Lot halls. The bank is a function of the Cast Lot and its halls, not a separate organization.

A crowned six-sided die hangs above the main doors, each pip picked out in gold. Varo says every wager is decided twice: once when the lot is cast and again when the bettor accepts what stands.

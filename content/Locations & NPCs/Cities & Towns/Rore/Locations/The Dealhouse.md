[[The Dealhouse]] is the public civic office of [[Rore]], where petitions, records, licenses, and the city's public court are handled.

The ground floor contains petition desks, record rooms, public notices, and court chambers. Meeting rooms above are used by ward representatives, lenders, brokers, and commercial houses for public business.

The House Table is now understood as the senior council of [[The Cast Lot]], not a separate faction, and its principal meetings take place at [[The Crowned Die]] under [[Cassia Rook]].

The Dealhouse handles crimes, civic disputes, licenses, and public law. [[Salt Court]] remains separate: it is a private neutral venue for contracts, debts, wagers, and commercial arbitration.

No gaming tables are licensed inside the Dealhouse. Rore's government prefers to keep its metaphors from becoming evidence.

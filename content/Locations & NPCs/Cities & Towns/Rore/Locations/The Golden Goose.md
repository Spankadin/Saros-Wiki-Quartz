[[The Golden Goose]] is the largest and most famous gambling hall in Saros. It is [[Rore]]'s sole [[Gambler's Guild|Guild-chartered]] hall and also operates as a [[Bank of Saros]].

[[Varo Quill]] owns and operates the Goose. Its licensed tables, tournament floors, counting rooms, kitchens, guest suites, and secured vaults fill a gilt-fronted building that never completely closes.

## Gambling

The hall hosts public tables, professional tournaments, and private games whose stakes can include ships, businesses, and contracts. Guild rules protect the integrity of a declared wager; they do not protect a gambler from a foolish one.

## Banking

Like every chartered Guild hall, the Golden Goose is a Bank of Saros. It accepts deposits, honors verified Guild drafts, changes currency, stores valuables, and arranges withdrawals through other Guild halls.

The golden goose above the main doors is intentionally gaudy. Varo says anyone embarrassed by obvious bait should reconsider entering a gambling hall.

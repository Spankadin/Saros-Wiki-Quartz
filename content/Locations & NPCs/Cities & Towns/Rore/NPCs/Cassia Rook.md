[[Cassia Rook]] is the current First Dealer of [[Rore]], presiding over the House Table and the senior affairs of [[The Cast Lot]] from [[The Crowned Die]].

She is polished, patient, and almost impossible to embarrass in public.

Cassia insists that Rore is not lawless. It simply refuses to let the Crown decide which laws are worth having.

She governs the Lot's senior council and Rore's commercial coalition. [[Varo Quill]] runs the Crowned Die's daily hall operations, while Cassia handles factional policy, civic bargains, and disputes large enough to threaten the Lot as a whole.

She treats the [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] as a foreign military institution whenever its officers enter the city.

### Manner

Cassia is polished without being warm. She lets other people make threats, then asks what outcome they actually expect. In Rore, that ability to separate pride from business is one reason she remains First Dealer.

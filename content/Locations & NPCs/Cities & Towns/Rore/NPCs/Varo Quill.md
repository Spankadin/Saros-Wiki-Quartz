[[Varo Quill]] owns and operates [[The Crowned Die]], the largest and most famous gambling hall in [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]]. He is one of the most visible representatives of [[The Cast Lot]] in [[Rore]].

He sponsors tournaments, arranges high-stakes private games, and knows which visitors are gambling for pleasure and which are gambling because they desperately need money.

Varo is friendly to anyone who can afford to lose.

## Personality

### Manner

Varo treats conversation like a table game. He enjoys risk, notices desperation quickly, and prefers people who understand exactly what they are wagering. He is rarely the loudest person in a room, but he often knows who is losing before they do.

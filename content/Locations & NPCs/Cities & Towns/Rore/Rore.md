[[Rore]] is the largest openly independent city in eastern Saros and a place where nearly anything can be wagered, financed, sold, hidden, or made respectable after enough paperwork and coin.

The Crown calls Rore unruly. Rore calls itself free.

## Character

The city is bright, noisy, cynical, and surprisingly organized. The Guild's single chartered hall competes with unsanctioned house games and street games, lenders advertise beside taverns, private guards watch every profitable doorway, and a person can lose a year's wages before breakfast if determined enough.

Rore's independence attracts people who dislike Crown oversight as well as people who simply prefer looser rules.

## Government

[[The Cast Lot|The House Table]] governs the city as the senior council of [[The Cast Lot]]. Great gaming houses, lenders, brokers, and ward representatives dominate its bargains, but it is no longer treated as a separate faction.

[[Cassia Rook]] serves as **First Dealer**, the closest office Rore accepts to a mayor.

There is no permanent [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] garrison inside Rore. Crown officers may enter on official business, but the city does not recognize their authority over ordinary local civil law.

## Trade and Economy

Rore earns money through:

- Gambling and gaming tourism
- Lending and debt purchase
- Coastal trade
- Contraband brokerage
- Entertainment
- Private auctions
- Money changing
- Contract arbitration
- Resale of cargo whose ownership has become inconvenient

The Lot rules through negotiation rather than simple command. Ward interests, lenders, brokers, and independent houses can still defeat a proposal when Cassia cannot assemble a profitable coalition.

## Travel and Connections

The road to [[Trystn]] is a major long-distance corridor of roughly **150-170 miles**: 8-9 travel days walking, 10-12 by heavy wagon, 5-6 days mounted, or 1.5-2 days by griffin.

Heavy trade with [[Ari]] usually prefers the coast. The journey takes about 1 day by coastal trader in favorable weather, compared with 4-5 days walking, 5-7 by heavy wagon, or about 3 days mounted.

See [[Saros - Distances, Travel, Trade, and Population]] for the canonical route model.

## Security

Rore is policed by ward guards funded by the major houses. Casinos, warehouses, lenders, and brokers also employ private guards.

Violence that interferes with business is punished quickly. Violence between people who knowingly entered dangerous arrangements receives much less sympathy.

## Places of Interest

- [[The Crowned Die]] — Saros's largest gambling hall, the headquarters of [[The Cast Lot]], and Rore's legitimate bank
- [[The Dealhouse]] — public civic offices, records, petition hall, and court
- [[The Black Ledger]] — private brokerage house operated by [[Nessa Crowe]]
- [[Salt Court]] — neutral arbitration hall for debts, contracts, and wagers

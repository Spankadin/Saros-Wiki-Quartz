A Temple dedicated to Austerus "The Shining One" located in [[Skaggerty]]. Deity of redemption and holy light, formerly the fiendish deity of searing radiance, now turned to Order. Seeks to bring all of humanity under one rule.

[[Heldoren The Wise]] oversees the daily operations of the Temple.

## Local Importance

The place is a familiar part of daily life and a useful point of reference for visitors. Its value comes from the ordinary work, meetings, and routines that happen there rather than from a single famous event.

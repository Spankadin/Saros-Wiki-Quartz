Ran by [[Simoon]], a diminutive and unassuming human male with quite an intellect and a dark sense of humor. He lives above the Stout Fool in one of the only few available rooms.

**Rooms:** (per night)
Modest (1 sp) = 3
Accommodating (5 sp) = 1

## Everyday Use

The establishment serves as more than a place to drink or sleep. Travelers use it to hear road conditions, find temporary help, arrange meetings, and learn which local businesses are actually open to strangers.

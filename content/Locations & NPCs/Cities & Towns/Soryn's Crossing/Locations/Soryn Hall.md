[[Soryn Hall]] contains the toll offices and council rooms of [[Soryn's Crossing]]. [[Dalen Soryn]] conducts most public business here.

## Public Use

Most residents encounter the building through ordinary civic business rather than ceremony. Notices, disputes, permits, records, and requests for local officials all pass through it, making the place a useful measure of what the settlement currently considers important.

## Records

The Hall maintains toll books, warehouse licenses, caravan declarations, and road-maintenance accounts.

Because so much freight passes through [[Soryn's Crossing]], old records can reveal when a merchant, wagon company, or cargo route first began appearing in the south.

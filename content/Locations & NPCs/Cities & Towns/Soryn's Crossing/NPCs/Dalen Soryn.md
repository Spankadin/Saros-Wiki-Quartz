[[Dalen Soryn]] is First Warden of [[Soryn's Crossing]] and a distant descendant of the family associated with the settlement's modern name.

Dalen is a merchant-administrator more than a noble. He understands toll schedules, warehouse capacity, and caravan contracts better than ceremony.

He is usually willing to make a deal if the result keeps traffic moving through the Crossing.

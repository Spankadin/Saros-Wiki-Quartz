[[The Golden Goose]] is [[Trystn]]'s sole hall chartered by [[The Cast Lot]] and the town's legitimate bank. It stands near [[Four Roads Market]], where caravan traffic and four converging roads keep its tables, counting rooms, and secure draft office busy at nearly every hour.

## Gaming Hall

The Goose favors games that resolve cleanly between arrivals and departures: dice, cards, racing books, caravan pools, and declared wagers on road conditions. Longer private games occupy quieter rooms above the main floor.

## Banking

Merchants use the hall to deposit coin, exchange currency, secure valuables, and arrange verified withdrawals through other Lot halls. Its position at the crossroads makes it especially useful to travelers changing caravans or dividing cargo among several routes.

## Appearance

A gilded goose with a die held in its beak marks the entrance. Local teamsters say the bird watches every wagon leave and remembers which drivers claimed to be poor on the way in.

[[Tollhouse Commons]] houses road records, public notices, toll disputes, and the offices of [[Elian Thorne]] and the [[Crossroads Council]].

Large route maps cover the walls. Colored pins mark closures, monster sightings, missing caravans, and damaged bridges.

## Local Importance

The place is a familiar part of daily life and a useful point of reference for visitors. Its value comes from the ordinary work, meetings, and routines that happen there rather than from a single famous event.

[[Elian Thorne]] is the Roadwarden of [[Trystn]] and chairs the [[Crossroads Council]].

He is less interested in ceremony than road conditions, bridge timbers, grain prices, and whether the next caravan will arrive before the last one consumes the available stable space.

Elian maintains working relationships with merchants from [[Port Quartz]], miners from [[Lilya]], and gamblers from [[Rore]] without trusting any of them completely.

### Manner

Elian has the habit of asking where a wagon is going before asking who owns it. His priorities are movement, safety, and keeping one merchant's urgency from blocking an entire road.

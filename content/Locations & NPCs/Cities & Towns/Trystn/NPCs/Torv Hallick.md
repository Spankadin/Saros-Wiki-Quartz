[[Torv Hallick]] is a caravan master based at [[East Gate Caravan Yard]] in [[Trystn]].

He organizes freight east toward [[Rore]], west toward [[Port Quartz]], and north toward [[Lilya]].

Torv has recently seen unusually heavy sealed cargo traveling under official protection with paperwork too vague for its weight.

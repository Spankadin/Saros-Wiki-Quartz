Recently re-discovered and unsealed by an unknown source, a dwarf at [[The Toad|The Toad]] has been leading adventurers into the cave entrance to the [[Delves|Delve]] under the assumption that his original party is all but lost down there.

A party of adventurers thwarted [[The Prism]]'s attempt to open the 2nd level of the delve that was sealed by magic centuries ago.

## Access

The known entrance lies close enough to [[Goslow]] that the site has been explored repeatedly, but only the upper portions can be called familiar.

Collapsed passages, later occupants, and older sealed sections make every map incomplete.

## Importance

The delve is one reason scholars and adventurers in Goslow take dwarven ruins seriously rather than treating them as distant mountain stories.

Objects recovered from the complex occasionally reach specialists such as [[Bedford]] or the library at [[Temple of Torm]].

[[Port Quartz Delve]] is a known [[Delves|dwarven delve]] beneath or immediately adjacent to [[Port Quartz]].

Unlike many rediscovered delves, it is not open to casual adventuring.

## Control

The accessible entrance is heavily guarded.

The [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] provides much of the physical security, while the legal right to admit independent explorers remains a source of civic sensitivity in Port Quartz.

Visitors normally require recognized authorization before entering.

## Reputation

Very little reliable information about the deeper interior is available publicly.

That secrecy has produced predictable rumors of treasure, sealed dwarven machinery, dangerous lower passages, and discoveries the authorities do not want ordinary people to see.

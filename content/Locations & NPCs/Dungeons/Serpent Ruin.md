[[Serpent Ruin]] is the ruined underground city and burial complex of [[The Serpent King]], located several miles south of [[Goslow]].

The site once belonged to a prosperous snake-man realm.

Time, collapse, occupation, and failed magical work have turned it into a dangerous ruin.

## Known Dangers

Stories and returning adventurers have associated the complex with:

- Goblins
- Blue mushrooms
- Unstable chambers
- Old snake-man architecture
- Traps
- The lingering work of [[Zixu]]

## Reputation

The ruin is close enough to Goslow that would-be adventurers can reach it without mounting a major expedition.

That accessibility has made it famous.

It has not made it safe.

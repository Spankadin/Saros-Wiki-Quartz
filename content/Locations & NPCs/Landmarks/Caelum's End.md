[[Caelum's End]] is the northernmost point of Saros that can be reached on foot. The frozen peak ends at a sheer drop into the northern sea.

## The Confrontation

Public histories agree on only a few facts.

[[Caelum]], [[Queen Amara]], and [[Silas Voss]] were present. No one but Amara witnessed what happened.

Caelum and Amara made a final attempt to bring their former pupil back from the brink of evil.

Caelum and Silas both went over the edge.

Amara returned alone to [[Pyrris]], where she assumed her husband's throne.

Neither Caelum nor Silas has been reliably seen since. Amara has never provided a complete public account, so whether either fall was accident, sacrifice, or attack—and whether either man survived—remains disputed.

## The Common Account

Most Sarosians tell a simpler and more violent story: [[Caelum]] pursued his fallen pupil across the north, master and student fought until they reached the end of the world, and both plunged from the cliffs during their final struggle.

Court histories insist that [[Queen Amara|Amara]] was present and that the journey began as one last attempt to turn Silas away from evil. Because Amara is the only known witness and has never told the complete story, neither version can be proven.

## Modern Importance

The remote peak became a historical and almost religious landmark despite its isolation.

Travelers who reach it find a cold, exposed region far removed from the political centers that still argue about what happened there.

The uncertainty surrounding Caelum's final confrontation has only made the place more significant.

[[The Masys Mountains]] form the remote northern range beyond the settled roads west and northwest of [[Port Quartz]]. At Hex 53.85, [[Dun Karag]] guards the maintained passage through the range toward [[Caelum's End]].

Wide rivers, hills, broken forest, and difficult crossings isolate much of the region from the rest of northern Saros.

## Travel

There is no dependable road from Port Quartz into the western Masys country.

Explorers normally begin by crossing at [[Westwater Ferry]] and following portions of [[Surveyors' Step]] before the trails disappear.

## Reputation

The region is associated with:

- Lost expeditions
- Old ruins
- Strange magical weather
- Hidden valleys
- Recent reports of sick or altered forest
- The modern legend of [[Rom The Wizard|Rom]] and a moving hill
- [[The Greyweald]], the haunted and fey-corrupted forest along the range's eastern side

The Rom stories are comparatively new—roughly the last eighty years—and do not appear in centuries-old expedition records such as those of [[Edric Vane]].

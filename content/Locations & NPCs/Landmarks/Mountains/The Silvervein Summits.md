[[The Silvervein Summits]] rise above [[Lilya]] and the high basin of [[Lake of Lament]].

[[The Greyweald]] spreads west of the range, filling the haunted country between the Summits and [[The Masys Mountains]].

The range is rich in mineral deposits and riddled with natural caves, worked mines, and older passages whose builders are not always known.

## Lilya

The mining town of Lilya exists because of the Summits.

Its economy depends on ore, stone, and materials pulled from the mountain, while its roads carry most normal freight south through [[Trystn]].

## Old Workings

Dwarven construction has been found beneath and around active mines.

Some passages appear to belong to systems far older and deeper than current Sarosian mining.

How much of the range has truly been explored is impossible to say.

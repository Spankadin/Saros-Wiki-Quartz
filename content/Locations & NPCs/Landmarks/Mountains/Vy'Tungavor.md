[[Vy'Tungavor]] is a rugged eastern mountain region feeding rivers toward the southern interior.

The lower slopes contain scattered mineral claims supplied in part through [[Ari]].

## Mining

Most operations near Vy'Tungavor are small compared with [[Lilya]].

Prospectors establish temporary camps, work promising veins, and abandon them quickly when the cost of guards, food, or transport exceeds the value of the ore.

## Travel

The region is broken, steep, and poorly patrolled beyond the main approaches.

Old caves and ruined workings make it attractive to adventurers as well as miners.

## Griffins

The high peaks are the traditional origin of Sarosian [[Griffin Riders]]. A prospective rider normally enters the mountains to claim a fresh hatchling and begin the lifelong pair-bond there.

Unhatched eggs are exceptionally rare. The mountains' scale, weather, predators, and inaccessible nesting country make every successful claiming dangerous, and many candidates return without a companion.

The tradition is not exclusive to any faction, although the [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]] fields the most publicly visible riders.

The [[Surveyors' Step]] is the collective name used in [[Port Quartz]] for the fading trails, cairns, hunter paths, and old expedition cuts beyond [[Westwater Ferry]].

It is not a true road.

The Step only describes the first portion of the northwest route where generations of travelers have left enough signs that one can pretend a path exists.

A cracked expedition marker connected to [[Edric Vane]] stands near the last dependable portion.

Beyond that point, travelers heading toward [[The Masys Mountains]] are navigating wilderness rather than following a road.

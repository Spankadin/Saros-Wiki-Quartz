[[The Glimmering Expanse]] is the vast central desert separating much of northern and southern [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]].

The region is not an ordinary sand desert.

Its dunes and flats are littered with razor-edged glass, fused sheets, crystalline ridges, and enormous formations that rise from the ground like broken transparent stone.

## First Age Scar

Sarosian tradition holds that the Expanse was created during the dragon wars of the [[Ages of Saros|First Age]].

The nearby [[The Wyrm Scar|Wyrm Scar]] is considered the clearest physical evidence that those stories may describe a real catastrophe.

Whatever happened transformed an enormous region into a place where heat, wind, sand, and glass can destroy an unprepared expedition.

## Travel

Crossing the Expanse is considered one of the most dangerous overland journeys in Saros.

Hazards include:

- Extreme heat
- Lack of dependable water
- Glass storms driven by high winds
- Shifting dunes
- Jagged fields that injure animals
- Poor landmarks
- Magical distortions
- Long stretches without shelter

Few living guides will publicly claim they can cross the region and return.

## The Land Bridge

A narrow region of traversable land connects the northern and southern halves of the continent across the larger wound of [[The Wyrm Scar]].

On a map this can look like an obvious route.

In practice, reaching and crossing it requires surviving the Expanse itself, and the route is considered nearly unusable.

## Dun'armok

Old stories claim the dwarven [[Dun'armok]] once knew safe routes through the Expanse and may even have lived within it.

Modern Sarosians generally treat claims that Dun'armok communities still survive there as tall tales.

## The Great Sphinx

Another persistent legend places [[The Great Sphinx]] somewhere within the Expanse.

No dependable map agrees on where.

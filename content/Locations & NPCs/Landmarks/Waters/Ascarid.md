The [[Ascarid]] begins where [[Lake of Lament]] plunges over [[Great Fall]] at hex **89.84**. Its Elvish name means **“Rushing-waters.”**

The river carries the [[Fall Casks|fall-cask trade]] past [[North Cataract Yard]] and [[South Cataract Yard]] before meeting the [[Dun'Nala]] at [[Port Quartz]]. Its current remains swift even in broad reaches, though the river narrows and becomes comparatively calm enough for the Cataract Yards to work at hexes **82.94** and **81.95**.

Below the confluence, the joined river is called [[The Coldwater]].

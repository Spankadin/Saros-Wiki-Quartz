[[Bishop's Bay]] is the broad sheltered water shared by [[Goslow]] and the towering cliffs beneath [[Pyrris]].

Its geography is one of the main reasons Goslow became the largest commercial city in Saros.

## Goslow

The [[Breakwater Fingers]] shelter Goslow's inner docks but block every easy-looking direct approach. The only generally safe route into Goslow enters from the west and follows a channel known to local pilots.

Large vessels wait for tide and weather, take on a pilot, or anchor farther out while lighters finish cargo and passenger runs. The difficult entrance supports signal crews, pilots, towboats, salvage workers, and chandlers.

## Pyrris

The cliffs beneath [[Pyrris]] rise almost sheer from the bay.

The waters nearest the capital are heavily watched by the Crown and [[The Aegis Legion|Aegis Legion]], and ordinary vessels are discouraged from approaching the protected cliffside.

## Importance

Bishop's Bay links royal power and commercial power more directly than almost any other body of water in Saros:

- [[Goslow]] supplies trade and daily necessities
- [[Pyrris]] supplies law, ceremony, and administration
- [[The High Road]] connects them over land

The [[Breakwater Fingers]] are the great rock formations reaching into [[Bishop's Bay]] west and south of [[Goslow]]. They blunt the bay's heavy currents and keep the city's inner docks calm enough for many kinds of vessel.

The same rocks make entry dangerous. Goslow can be approached safely only from the west through a channel that bends between shoals and submerged stone. A vessel leaving Goslow cannot simply turn south toward [[Pyrris]]; it must first clear the Fingers.

Local pilots, signal stations, tide windows, towboats, lighters, and salvage crews have grown into trades of their own. Captains who decline a pilot are remembered mostly when their wreckage reaches shore.

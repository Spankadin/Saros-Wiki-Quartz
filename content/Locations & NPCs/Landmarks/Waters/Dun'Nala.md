The [[Dun'Nala]] descends from the northern [[The Masys Mountains|Masys Mountains]] and runs southeast toward [[Port Quartz]]. Its name means **“the Dwarves' Water”** in Sarosian Dwarvish; **Dun** is pronounced “dune.”

On Perla's map it is one of the realm's predominant waterways, broad enough in many reaches to behave like an inland sea. Mountain runoff makes its current cold, deep, and seasonally violent.

At Port Quartz, the Dun'Nala joins the [[Ascarid]]. The combined river flowing south is called [[The Coldwater]].

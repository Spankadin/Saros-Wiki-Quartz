[[Fallhead Station]] is the small working outpost at hex **89.83** above [[Great Fall]]. Broad-beamed boats from [[Lilya]] unload casks, crews inspect their seals and balance, and capstan teams transfer them into the protected fall works.

The station contains bunkrooms, a cooper's shed, a signal tower, stores, cranes, and storm shelters rather than a permanent village. Its workers know the currents of [[Lake of Lament]] and the condition of every release cable by sound.

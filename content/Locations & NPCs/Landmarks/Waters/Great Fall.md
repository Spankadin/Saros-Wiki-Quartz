[[Great Fall]] is the immense cataract at hex **89.84** where [[Lake of Lament]] becomes the [[Ascarid]]. No ordinary vessel can descend it intact or climb it at all.

The [[Fall Casks|fall-cask trade]] uses a protected basin, chain booms, guide cables, capstans, and shore crews to feed reinforced casks into controlled release lanes. Boats unload at [[Fallhead Station]] rather than approaching the brink.

Mist, spray, loose rock, sudden wind, and the thunder of falling water make the works dangerous even on a good day.

[[Lake of Lament]] occupies the high basin around [[The Silvervein Summits]].

Beyond its northern shore lies [[The Greyweald]], whose grey treeline fills the country between the Silvervein Summits and [[The Masys Mountains]].

It sits far above the elevation of [[Port Quartz]], fed by mountain water and drained through several channels.

## The Great Fall

At hex **89.84** on the lake's southwestern edge, the water plunges over [[Great Fall]] and becomes the [[Ascarid]], flowing toward [[Port Quartz]]. A small fallhead station at **89.83** stages cargo above the release works.

The fall makes ship travel between Port Quartz and the lake impossible.

No vessel can climb it.

The violent downstream route is nevertheless used for one unusual kind of cargo: reinforced [[Fall Casks]].

## Trade

Broad-beamed working boats and rafts carry fuel, ore, and sealed casks between [[Lilya]] at **106.81** and the fallhead. The roughly **85-mile** passage usually takes a loaded convoy 3–5 days. Crews use sheltered landings in bad mountain weather rather than risk the outlet currents.

Ordinary passengers and fragile freight still travel by road through [[Trystn]], but the long road and recurring closures make the cask route economically necessary.

## Reputation

The lake is beautiful from a distance and dangerous at its edges.

Mountain weather can change water conditions quickly, and the outlet currents become increasingly violent toward the waterfall.

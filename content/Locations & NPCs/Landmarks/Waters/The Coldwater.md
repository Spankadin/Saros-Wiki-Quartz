[[The Coldwater]] is the common Sarosian name for the great river formed where the [[Dun'Nala]] and [[Ascarid]] meet at [[Port Quartz]], hex **79.96**. From there it flows south toward [[Pyrris]] and empties into [[Bishop's Bay]].

The Coldwater is one of northern Saros's essential trade corridors and one of its greatest barriers. Its broad, deep current makes bridging impractical. Upstream ships use sail, cordelling, warping, kedging, towing boats, and the prepared hauling works maintained around Port Quartz.

The ordinary safe voyage between [[Goslow]] and Port Quartz is roughly **135–150 miles**. It is never a guaranteed shortcut: current, wind, shoals, traffic, weather, and the difficult entrance through the [[Breakwater Fingers]] all matter.

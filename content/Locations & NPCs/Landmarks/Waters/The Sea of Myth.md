[[The Sea of Myth]] lies along the western side of [[A Brief Saros History|Saros]].

Its name reflects how little certainty Sarosians possess about what lies far beyond familiar coastal routes.

[[Kylor]], [[Goslow]], and other western settlements depend on coastal waters connected to the Sea for fishing and trade.

Sailors tell stories of distant islands, enormous creatures, strange weather, and lights beyond known charts.

Few captains are wealthy or reckless enough to test every story.
